# Glossary

* [Adapt copy](adapt-copy.md) - An adapt copy: the copy of a published LinkML schema that `lokf adapt` writes so that it can sit beside LOKF's `lokf.yaml` in a domain schema, with its chosen roots on `Concept`.
* [Generative AI (GAI)](generative-ai.md) - The class of AI models that emulate the structure and characteristics of input data to generate derived synthetic content. The publication says GAI generally refers to generative foundation models.
* [GAI risk](gai-risk.md) - One of the 12 risks that section 2 defines as unique to or made worse by the development and use of GAI, such as Confabulation or Information Integrity.
* [Suggested action](suggested-action.md) - A step an organization or AI actor can take to manage GAI risks, named by an Action ID such as GV-1.1-001. Section 3 lists 212.
* [Primary consideration](primary-consideration.md) - One of four themes from NIST's Generative AI Public Working Group: Governance, Pre-Deployment Testing, Content Provenance and Incident Disclosure.
* [AI red-teaming](ai-red-teaming.md) - A structured testing exercise that probes an AI system for flaws and vulnerabilities, often in a controlled environment and with its developers. Section A.1.5 names four types.
* [Provenance data tracking](provenance-data-tracking.md) - Recording the origin and history of digital content, through metadata and digital watermarks, so its authenticity can be determined. Section A.1.6 names four techniques.
* [AI incident](ai-incident.md) - An event where developing, using or a malfunction of AI systems contributes to one of four kinds of harm, by the definition section A.1.8 quotes. An incident can be aggregate or acute.
