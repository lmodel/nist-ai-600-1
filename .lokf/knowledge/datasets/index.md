# Datasets

* [Generated artefacts](generated-artefacts.md) - What `just gen-project` and `just gen-doc` generate from the schema: the projections in `project/`, the Python models in `src/nist_ai_600_1/datamodel/`, and the merged schema in `docs/schema/`. None is edited by hand.
* [Neighbours' adapt copies](neighbour-copies.md) - The adapt copies of 19 released lmodel repositories in `tests/neighbours/`, among them oscal, stix and spdx, each pinned by its SHA-256, and lmodel/nist-ai-100-1's copy from its branch. `tests/test_schema_rules.py` holds this schema's copy apart from them and checks the mappings.
* [Example data](example-data.md) - The examples `tests/test_data.py` checks: a valid example of each class that is not abstract, and a counter-example for each constraint, whose first line names where it must fail.
* [nist-ai-600-1 LinkML schema](nist-ai-600-1-linkml-schema.md) - The LinkML model of NIST AI 600-1, `src/nist_ai_600_1/schema/nist_ai_600_1.yaml`: an umbrella over three modules and the vendored AI RMF slice that holds the tree root `GaiProfile`, with the `Gai` class prefix and the `gai_` slot prefix.
* [Risks module](risks-module.md) - `nist_ai_600_1_risks.yaml`: `GaiRisk`, the enum of 12 risks with the tables' spellings as aliases, the groupings of footnote 5, and the lifecycle, scope, source and time-scale values of section 2.
* [Actions module](actions-module.md) - `nist_ai_600_1_actions.yaml`: `GaiSubcategory`, one table of section 3, and `GaiSuggestedAction`, one row with its Action ID, text and risk tags. AI actor tasks belong to the table.
* [Considerations module](considerations-module.md) - `nist_ai_600_1_considerations.yaml`: a class for each of Appendix A's four primary considerations, the kinds of structured public feedback, and the lists of governance plans, third-party controls, provenance techniques and incident harms.
* [Vendored AI RMF modules](vendored-modules.md) - `src/nist_ai_600_1/schema/nist_ai_100_1/`: the base and profiles modules of lmodel/nist-ai-100-1, unchanged and pinned by SHA-256 in `tests/test_upstream.py`, so the schema needs no network.
* [The catalogue](catalogue.md) - `src/nist_ai_600_1/data/ai-600-1.yaml`, the whole Profile as one `GaiProfile` record: 12 risks, 49 tables, 212 suggested actions and four primary considerations.
* [Copy of the upstream](upstream-copy.md) - `upstream/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf`: the publication, unchanged, which `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` pins by SHA-256 and `tests/test_upstream.py` compares offline.
* [The schema's adapt copy](adapt-copy.md) - `.lokf/schema/nist-ai-600-1.yaml`, the copy `just lokf-adapt` writes from the schema so that it sits beside LOKF's `lokf.yaml`: five classes rooted on `Concept`, `ai_rmf_id` dropped for LOKF's `id`, `title` and `description` folded into LOKF's, and two designators demoted.
* [Confabulation](example-risk.md) - The risk of confabulation, written as a concept of type `GaiRisk` in the adapt copy's keys. It shows that the domain schema admits a risk as a concept, with the keys an adopter fills in.
